## Frequently asked questions

### Can Core G3 fill both cans and bottles?

Yes. Sensor mode is intended for cans. Bottles are filled in timer mode and need to be held in position while filling.

### Is DuoFiller a counter-pressure filler?

No. DuoFiller is an open filler. It pre-purges the container with CO₂ and then fills from a pressurised keg, unitank or similar vessel.

### What pressure should I use?

Purge-gas pressure must never exceed **3 psi / 0.2 bar**. Beverage pressure must remain between **7 and 20 psi / 0.5 and 1.4 bar**. Follow the official manual for the complete setup.

### How do I identify the right spare part?

Check the generation and serial number on your filler. Include both when contacting Brewket so compatibility can be confirmed before ordering.
