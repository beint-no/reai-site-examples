## Cart data

Cart contents are stored in your browser so they remain available on the same device. The cart is sent to the ReAI checkout only when you choose to continue.

## Secure checkout

Customer, shipping and payment details are collected by ReAI during checkout. The storefront never receives or stores your card details.

## Contact

The contact form opens your own email application. Nothing is transmitted by the website before you review and send the message.

Questions about personal data can be sent to [post@brewket.no](mailto:post@brewket.no).
