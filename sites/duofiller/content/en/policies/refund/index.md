## Before returning an item

Contact us before sending anything back. We will confirm the return address and the instructions that apply to the order.

## Refunds

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method after the returned item has been received and checked. The order confirmation and applicable consumer law determine the final conditions.

## Contact

Use the [contact page](/contact/) and include the order number so we can help efficiently.
