The order confirmation identifies the products, variants, prices, shipping and applicable taxes. Contact Brewket immediately if anything is incorrect.

The customer is responsible for selecting the correct generation and connection unless Brewket has confirmed compatibility in writing. Include the filler model and serial number when ordering a service part.

## Technical information

Product pages are an overview and do not replace the installation, safety, cleaning or operating instructions supplied for the exact model. Purge-gas pressure must never exceed **3 psi / 0.2 bar**.
