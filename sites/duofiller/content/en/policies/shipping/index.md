## Shipping from Norway

DuoFiller products are dispatched from Skjold, Norway. Available carriers, delivery cost and estimated delivery time depend on the destination and order contents and are shown before payment.

## International orders

Import duties, taxes and customs handling may apply outside Norway. These charges are normally collected by the carrier or local authorities and are not included unless checkout explicitly says otherwise.

## Damage or missing parcels

Inspect the parcel on delivery. Keep the packaging and contact [post@brewket.no](mailto:post@brewket.no) promptly if the shipment is visibly damaged or does not arrive as expected.
