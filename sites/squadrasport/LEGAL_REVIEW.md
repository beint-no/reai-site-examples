# Squadra Sport legal-page review

The Norwegian privacy, return, and sales-term pages are site-owned drafts in `legal-pages.mjs`. They replace preview placeholders but are not merchant-approved terms. The public domain and checkout are live at the merchant’s request. Merchant approval, the physical return address, and the checkout disclosure still need confirmation.

## Confirm with Squadra Sport AS

- **Addresses:** The current Shopify contact page lists Stasjonsveien 10, 1365 Blommenholm. [Brønnøysundregistrene](https://virksomhet.brreg.no/nb/oppslag/enheter/936175260) lists Per Waalers vei 24, 1360 Fornebu as the registered business address. The drafts use the registered address for seller identity. Confirm the customer contact address and the physical return address as soon as possible.
- **Returns:** The draft says the customer pays direct return shipping and asks customers to obtain the return address by email. Confirm both points, and provide the actual return address as soon as possible. Confirm whether any products are genuinely made to customer specifications or have a clear personal character; if so, identify them and present any lawful exception before purchase. Choosing a standard size or colour alone is not such an exception.
- **Payment and delivery:** Verify the payment methods, order-confirmation email, available destinations, delivery estimates, and selected carriers in a complete test order. The pages deliberately do not promise a fixed delivery time or a payment method not shown at checkout.
- **Privacy:** Confirm the legal entities and roles of ReAI, Adyen, Cloudflare, and the carriers; any transfers outside the EEA and their safeguards; and actual retention periods for technical logs and customer correspondence. Update the draft if those facts differ. Check whether marketing or analytics tracking is active.
- **Checkout disclosure:** Ensure the buyer can access these terms, the withdrawal information, and a standard withdrawal form before purchase, and receives the required information with the order confirmation. The return page contains a copyable withdrawal form for the preview.
- **Approval:** Have the merchant approve the final Norwegian wording and publish an effective date.
