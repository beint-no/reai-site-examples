# Asset provenance

The demo's gift, mug, heart, cloud, confetti, sun and geometric mark are original
SVG illustrations authored for this repository. They depict fictional demo
products and are supplied under MIT. The optional setup command rasterizes them
and uploads them to ReAI, which serves the public product media and renditions.
The deployed Worker never imports the seed catalog as a live data fallback.

The Studio, Atelier and Supply design photographs are original AI-generated
illustrations, including the scenario color variants. They depict no actual
stock or client business and are supplied under MIT.

The starter's mark is a generic SVG; its editorial hero was generated for the
generic ReAI storefront starter and does not depict a merchant or actual stock.
Keep the MIT source attribution when exporting the starter.

Real merchant content and ReAI-hosted media remain governed by their owners'
rights. The repository license grants no rights to customer assets. Do not add other customer names, photographs, logos or private content without
explicit permission.

## Authorized Famme design-test photography

Greg explicitly authorized using photographs from his/family-owned famme.no in
this public ReAI test store on 2026-10-10, including conversion to AVIF.
`demo/public/assets/famme/` contains those resized conversions. Sources, output
dimensions and byte sizes are recorded in `sources.json`. Photographs and the
Famme name remain Famme-owned; the repository MIT license does not license them
for general reuse. The standalone starter exporter does not include demo assets.

The Famme design is implemented from scratch. No Shopify/Famme HTML, theme code,
scripts, fonts, reviews, marketing signup offers or customer data were copied.
Its clothing catalog, prices, collections and stock are illustrative UI fixtures,
clearly labeled and isolated from live payment.
